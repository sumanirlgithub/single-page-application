One important SEPA distinction

At this point, your processor is not yet sending a real SEPA payment. It is receiving the direct-debit instruction and creating an internal payment record.

The next logical step is to implement:

SEPA_PAYMENT
     |
     v
Build pain.008 XML
     |
     v
Validate XML
     |
     v
Send to Bank / PSP
     |
     v
Update status

For a real SEPA Direct Debit flow, the customer-to-bank initiation message is generally ISO 20022 pain.008, rather than simply sending your internal JSON event to the bank. The Kafka event is your internal microservice event; pain.008 is the external SEPA payment message.

So I would keep those two layers separate:

Kafka JSON
   ↓
DirectDebitCreatedEvent
   ↓
SEPA Payment Processor
   ↓
pain.008 XML
   ↓
Bank / PSP

That separation will make your project much closer to a real banking/payment architecture.
